
# Isolation and Loneliness

Isolation and loneliness are common themes in horror movies. When characters are separated from other people, they can feel trapped, helpless and more vulnerable. This can make the audience feel nervous because there is no one around to help when something goes wrong.

## How Isolation Creates Fear

Horror movies often place characters in locations where it is difficult to get help. This works by exploring how the human mind breaks when stripped of meaningful connection.

#### Some common examples are:
- being alone in a remote house
- getting stranded in the wilderness 
- losing contact with family members 
- being trapped somewhere with no way out

### Example: *The Shining*

The shining creates fear because it suggests that the most terrifying monster is not a n external intruder, but the person we become when we are left entirely alone with our thoughts. This shows how isolation can make a horror movie more intense.
